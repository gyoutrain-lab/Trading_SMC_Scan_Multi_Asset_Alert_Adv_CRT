Seconds Desk · Gold & Silver (live ticks → 1–30 s candles)

Candle size
Seconds candles only time the entry inside a zone the Swing Desk already approved (D1 + 4H direction, 4H zone, 1H break). They never choose direction: at this scale most moves are noise, so the engine demands a sweep, a real displacement, no spike and a stop the account can carry.

Swing Desk · Gold & Silver (D1 → 4H → 1H → 15m)

Swing rule: direction comes from D1 + 4H only; the 1H proves the reversal is real; the 15m only refines the entry and the stop. A smaller timeframe never decides direction, because below 1H gold noise creates most fake signals.

Metals Desk · Gold & Silver (separate rules)

News UTC
Direction lock = higher-timeframe trend agrees, the other metal does not contradict, and the dollar (UUP) is not fighting the trade. It cannot prove the future: it removes the setups that most often reverse.
